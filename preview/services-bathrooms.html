Bathroom refurbishments

Bathrooms stripped back to the walls and rebuilt properly. The services and the waterproofing first, then the tiling and the fittings.

A bathroom is a small room where everything is either wet or hidden, which is why it's the room most often paid for twice.

Taken back to the walls, the order is what matters: services and falls first, then boarding and waterproofing behind the tiles, then tiling, then fittings. Tile backer board and a proper tanking system in the wet areas cost very little at that stage, and they are the difference between a bathroom that lasts and one that quietly rots behind the grout.

The room comes back finished, including the parts people forget to price, like making good the ceiling and getting the extraction right.

What's included

  • Strip out and disposal
  • Re-routed plumbing and electrics
  • Tanking and tile backer board
  • Wall and floor tiling
  • Suite and screen fitting
  • Extraction and making good

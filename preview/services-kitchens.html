Kitchen refurbishments

Full kitchen refits, from stripping the old one out to a room that's ready to use, including the walls, floors and services hidden behind the units.

Most of a kitchen refit is invisible by the time it's finished. The old units come out, and what's behind them is usually where the real work is: walls that were never straight, pipework routed around a layout nobody uses any more, and sockets nowhere near the appliances you've actually bought.

That gets sorted before a single new unit goes back. Floors levelled, services moved to suit the new layout, walls made good. That way the units sit true, the doors line up, and the worktop meets the wall without a bead of filler doing the work.

Whether the kitchen itself comes from a supplier or from you, the room comes back finished rather than handed over to somebody else to tidy up.

What's included

  • Strip out and disposal
  • Layout changes and structural openings
  • Plumbing and electrics moved to suit
  • Floor levelling and tiling
  • Unit and worktop fitting
  • Making good and decorating

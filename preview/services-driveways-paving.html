Driveways & paving

Block paving, porcelain, natural stone, resin and tarmac. All laid on a dug-out, compacted base with proper falls, so it drains away from the house and stays flat.

The bit that decides whether a driveway is still flat in year five is the bit you never see. Ours go down on a geotextile membrane with 150mm or more of MOT Type 1, compacted in layers rather than in one pass, then a 30mm sharp sand laying course.

Falls are set so water runs away from the house and into a soakaway or a linear drain, not towards the door. Edge restraints are haunched properly, because a driveway that spreads at the edges has failed no matter how good the blocks were.

The corner that gets cut on a cheap quote is almost always the sub-base: 75mm instead of 150mm, uncompacted, no membrane. It looks perfect for two summers, then ruts under the car and the joints open up.

What's included

  • Block paved driveways
  • Porcelain and stone patios
  • Resin-bound surfacing
  • Drainage and soakaways

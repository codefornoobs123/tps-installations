Roofing & roofline

Full re-roofs, repairs, flat roofs and roofline. Stripped back, checked for rot, re-felted and battened properly. Not tiles laid back over a failing deck.

A cheap re-roof means tiles laid back over the existing felt and battens. It buys a few years and hides whatever is rotting underneath. We strip to the rafters, replace anything soft, and lay a breathable membrane across the full area before new graded battens go on.

Tiles can be new or reclaimed depending on what the property needs and what the conservation position is. Leadwork and flashings are formed properly rather than sealed with mastic and hoped for.

Flat roofs are EPDM or GRP, laid on a deck that's been checked rather than assumed. Fascias, soffits and guttering can be done at the same time while the scaffold is already up, which is usually the cheapest time to do them.

What's included

  • Full re-roofs
  • Tile and slate repairs
  • EPDM and GRP flat roofs
  • Fascias, soffits and guttering
  • Leadwork and flashing
  • Chimney and ridge work

Front driveways are in constant demand across Poole, from Parkstone to Broadstone to Canford Heath, where on-street parking gets harder every year.

Before we dig, we check the rules. Paving more than 5 square metres of a front garden in a surface water can't drain through needs planning permission. Permeable block paving, or directing water onto a planted area, keeps it within permitted development. A new or wider dropped kerb needs its own approval from the council.

Drainage matters more near the coast and harbour, where heavy rain has less room to go. We design the fall and the drainage so water goes where it should, not towards your house or your neighbour's.

Then it is the proper build up: dig out, compacted sub-base, edge restraints and the surface you've chosen.

Poole specifics: In Poole, dropped kerb applications go to BCP Council's highways team, and the kerb has to be lowered before a new driveway can be used.

Block paving, porcelain, natural stone, resin and tarmac. All laid on a dug-out, compacted base with proper falls, so it drains away from the house and stays flat.

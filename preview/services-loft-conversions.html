Loft conversions

Unused roof space turned into a room you'd actually want to be in. New floor structure, a proper staircase, insulation, and a dormer where the headroom calls for one.

A loft conversion is a structural job before it's a decorating one. The ceiling joists up there were sized to hold plasterboard, not people, so a new floor structure goes in independently of them. Everything else follows from that and from the headroom: where the stairs can land, whether the space needs a dormer, how the roof windows sit in the pitch.

So the first visit is spent in the loft with a tape measure rather than showing you a brochure. If the space works, you'll be told what it can become. If it doesn't, you'll be told that as well, before anyone has spent money finding out.

The person who prices it is the person who builds it, and the quote is a written fixed price with a programme you can plan around.

What's included

  • New floor structure
  • Dormers
  • Roof windows
  • Staircases
  • Insulation and plasterboard
  • Eaves storage

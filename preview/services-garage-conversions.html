Garage & outbuilding conversions

Space you already own, made properly habitable. An office, a spare room, a gym, finished to the same standard as the rest of the house.

The shell is usually the easy part. What decides whether a converted garage feels like a room or like a garage with carpet in it is what happens to the floor, the walls and the old door opening.

A garage floor is normally laid to fall towards the door so water runs out of it. That has to be levelled and insulated before anything else goes down. The walls are often single skin, so they get lined and insulated rather than simply skimmed. And the up-and-over opening is either built up in matching brick or replaced with doors or a window, depending on what the room is going to be used for.

Done properly it stops reading as a converted garage from the outside as well as the inside.

What's included

  • Garage conversions
  • Carport and outbuilding conversions
  • Insulated floors
  • Wall lining and insulation
  • Door openings built up or reglazed
  • Plastering and finishing
